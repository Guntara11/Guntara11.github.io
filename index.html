I joined to test SIM cards and found a job that touches everything: personalization, APDU validation, live 3G/4G/5G attach, OTA updates and eSIM lifecycle on SM-DP/SM-SR. As a senior I lead validation of eUICC, iSIM and IoT products.

  • Replaced manual workflows with OBTANA Web, the eUICC Provisioning Tool and the NVM Script Editor, turning 1–2 days of preparation into minutes.
  • Point of contact and trainer for interns across two intakes, and support for IDEMIA teams in Noida, France and Manila.
▶Full detail — what the role actually involved

Validation & test execution

  • Validate UICC/eUICC (SIM/eSIM) card OS and applets end to end on live 3G, 4G, 5G and NB-IoT networks for international mobile operators, down to APDU level with COMPRION tooling.
  • Run feasibility studies against 3GPP, ISO 7816, ETSI and GSMA SGP specifications, and decide what a product can and cannot be asked to do before the test plan is written.
  • Test eSIM remote provisioning against live SM-DP/SM-SR platforms (GSMA RSP), including profile download, enable/disable, and recovery from states the card and the platform disagree about.
  • Bring up test devices on Quectel, u-blox, Rollingwire and Nordic modules over AT commands, and analyse SIM traces and APDU exchanges when a failure is not where the log says it is.
  • As a senior, lead validation of the non-conventional products — eUICC, iSIM and IoT — where there is no existing test set to inherit.

Planning, reporting & process

  • Design and maintain test cases in Jira/Xray, plan the execution, and author the ATD and ATR documents that release a product.
  • Run regression across applet versions, card OS releases and modem configurations, and keep the test set honest — trimming coverage that no longer earns its runtime.
  • Work in Scrum and Kanban with the development and project teams, and report status to the people who decide whether a product ships.

Tooling & automation

  • NVM endurance automation — Python over AT commands running unattended 24-hour cycles on 5G modems, so engineers collect logs the next morning instead of sitting with the bench. Now used on roughly 90% of NVM projects.
  • eUICC Provisioning Tool — the team's main tool for eUICC projects. Cuts stuck-ICCID recovery from six manual steps across two tools to a few clicks (Django, PostgreSQL, corporate LDAP, Jenkins, Docker).
  • OBTANA, OTA test platform — pooled the shared 2G test handsets behind a browser tab (Flask, MQTT, Redis, Raspberry Pi), removing the fragile local Python setup every tester used to maintain.
  • NVM Script Editor and Validator — converts COMPRION trace logs into DLL-ready .pcom scripts, replacing up to three business days of manual editing, and corrected a length defect in all 22 GET IDENTITY commands of a ~20,000-command capture.

Mentoring & cross-site support

  • Point of contact and trainer for interns over two intake terms — onboarding, test method, and reviewing their work.
  • Support other IDEMIA sites (Noida, France and Manila) on applet testing, board flashing and module bring-up.

Industrial Voltage & Current Monitoring — power plant client

BLE Attendance Tracker — confidential client

  • Project lead, team of two, delivered as a working prototype.
  • Raspberry Pi scanner written from scratch in Python: identity from the iBeacon UUID rather than the randomised MAC address, enter/exit from an RSSI trend against a threshold, XML reports over FTP every 60 seconds.

WiFi Smart Plug — internal R&D
