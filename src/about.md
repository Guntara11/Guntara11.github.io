# About

I am an engineer who works at the line where software meets hardware, from two directions.

By day I am a senior QA engineer at **IDEMIA** in Jakarta, validating UICC and eUICC products — SIM and eSIM — for international mobile operators. That means reading the specifications a card has to meet, designing the tests, running them on live 3G, 4G, 5G and NB-IoT networks, and reading the APDU traffic between a device and a card to work out why something failed. I also built the tools my team tests with: the endurance automation, the OTA test platform, the eSIM provisioning tool, and the script generator that turns capture logs into runnable tests.

The rest of the time I build the devices themselves. With **Aligness**, an independent engineering team, I led projects from requirements through electronics, firmware and backend to the interface someone actually touches: greenhouse control panels running at five government sites, a flood and water-tank system at my parents' house, a monitoring dashboard for a power plant, and a Bluetooth attendance tracker for a client.

The write-ups on this site are not case studies. They say what the constraint was, what I decided, what it cost, and where the result falls short — because the failures are the part worth reading.

**Working with:** ESP32 (ESP-IDF, FreeRTOS), STM32, Raspberry Pi, C and C++, Python, MQTT, BLE, LVGL, PCB design, Django, Flask, Docker, and the GSMA RSP stack.
